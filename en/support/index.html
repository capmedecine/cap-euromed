Our support

From your plan to your admission, we're with you every step of the way

Cap EuroMed is with you from the first contact to settling into your new city. The first chat is free, with no commitment.

Step by step

How it works,
in practice.

Whether you're aiming for medicine, dentistry or veterinary medicine, the process is the same.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Free consultation on WhatsApp

    You get in touch, and we assess your profile, your grades and your motivation. We answer all your questions honestly.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Choosing a university and applying

    We help you choose the university that suits you and build a strong application: transcripts, motivation letters, translations.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Admission exam preparation

    Entrance exam, interview: depending on the university, we prepare you with targeted resources and practical advice.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Settling into your new city

    Housing, opening a bank account, registering your residence, health insurance: we help you settle in with peace of mind.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Support throughout your studies

    We stay available after your admission: advice, a network of French students on site, ongoing support.
