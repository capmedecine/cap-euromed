Frequently asked questions

Everything you're wondering.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll give you an honest answer.

Can you study medicine in Slovakia without the numerus clausus?

Yes. UPJŠ University in Košice, Slovakia, does not apply the French numerus clausus. Admission is based on your application and an entrance exam, with no PASS or LAS.

How does the UPJŠ entrance exam work?

It is a multiple-choice test in English covering biology, chemistry and physics. Each question has 4 options, of which 0 to 4 can be correct, and there are no negative points. The faculty usually holds one session in June and one in August: we give you your session dates and prepare you.

Is the UPJŠ medical degree recognised in France?

Yes. The UPJŠ medical degree is automatically recognised in France and throughout the European Union under Directive 2005/36/EC. As for students trained in France, you then complete a specialty (residency), which is required to practise as a GP or a specialist.

Can I do my residency in France?

Yes, it is possible. At the end of their studies, EU nationals can register for the EDN and then sit the ECOS, the same exams as students trained in France. You can also specialise in Slovakia or in another European country. The rules change over time: we go through the current situation with you when you plan your project.

How much does it cost to study medicine in Košice?

Tuition at UPJŠ Košice is €13,000 per year for medicine and €13,500 per year for dentistry. The cost of living in Košice is estimated at €630 to €890 per month.

Are medical courses at UPJŠ taught in English?

Yes. The full medicine and dentistry programmes at UPJŠ University in Košice are taught entirely in English, and are open to French-speaking students.
