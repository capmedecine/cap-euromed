Why choose this path?

Becoming a vet in Slovakia,
a serious alternative.

A degree recognised across the European Union

Your UVLF veterinary degree is automatically recognised in France and across the EU (Directive 2005/36/EC). To practise in France, you then register with the French veterinary council.

No French competitive exams

No French vet school exam. Admission is based on your application and a written biology and chemistry test, in English.

Taught in English

The whole veterinary programme is taught in English. A real asset for your CV and an international career, on top of a European degree.

Affordable cost of living

Košice is one of the cheapest cities in Europe. Housing, food, transport — your budget stays under control compared with Paris or Lyon.

Veterinary medicine

UVLF is the only university in Slovakia that trains vets. Its English programme lasts 6 years and is recognised at European level.

UVLF Košice —
Slovakia's veterinary school.

The University of Veterinary Medicine and Pharmacy in Košice (UVLF) is a Slovak public university founded in 1949. It is the only university in the country that trains vets, and it welcomes international students on its English programme.

Our team lives in Košice and is in touch with UVLF students: you won't be heading into the unknown.
