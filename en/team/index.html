Our team

The Cap EuroMed team: students who've been there

Cap EuroMed is a team of French medical and dental students already living in Košice. They welcome you on site and answer your practical questions. For veterinary medicine, we are also in touch with UVLF students.

On site, in Košice

What we do
for you.

We went through the same steps as you: choosing a university, getting in, settling in Košice. Now we help you get through them with peace of mind.

  • Help finding accommodation before you arrive, through trusted, already-vetted contacts.
  • Tips on exams, professors and how to get through the first years.
  • A welcome when you arrive — someone who speaks French, knows the city and can answer your practical questions.
  • A WhatsApp group of French students on site, so you never feel alone.
