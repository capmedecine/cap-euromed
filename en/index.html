Why go abroad?

Studying medicine abroad,
a real alternative.

A degree recognised across the European Union

A medical degree obtained in an EU country is automatically recognised in France (Directive 2005/36/EC). Then, just like students trained in France, you complete your specialty: in France through the residency exams, or elsewhere in Europe.

No PASS / LAS

No more numerus clausus lottery. Depending on the university, admission is based on your application, an entrance exam, or both.

Taught in English

The whole programme is taught in English. A real asset for your CV and an international career, on top of a European degree.

A lighter cost of living

In Central Europe, the cost of living is often far lower than in Paris or Lyon: housing, food, transport.

Medicine & dentistry

We support you towards both programmes, taught in English and recognised at European level.

Our support

From your plan
to your admission.

Cap EuroMed is with you at every step, from the first contact to settling into your new city.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Free consultation on WhatsApp

    You get in touch, and we assess your profile, your grades and your motivation. We answer all your questions honestly.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Choosing a university and applying

    We help you choose the university that suits you and build a strong application: transcripts, motivation letters, translations.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Admission exam preparation

    Entrance exam, interview: depending on the university, we prepare you with targeted resources and practical advice.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Settling into your new city

    Housing, opening a bank account, registering your residence, health insurance: we help you settle in with peace of mind.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Support throughout your studies

    We stay available after your admission: advice, a network of French students on site, ongoing support.
