Why go abroad?

Studying in Europe,
a real alternative.

A degree recognised across the European Union

Medical, dental and veterinary degrees obtained in an EU country are automatically recognised in France (Directive 2005/36/EC). In medicine, you then complete your specialty, just like students trained in France: through the residency exams in France, or elsewhere in Europe.

No French competitive exams

No PASS, no LAS, no French vet school exam. Depending on the university, admission is based on your application, an entrance exam, or both.

Taught in English

The whole programme is taught in English. A real asset for your CV and an international career, on top of a European degree.

A lighter cost of living

In Central Europe, the cost of living is often far lower than in Paris or Lyon: housing, food, transport.
