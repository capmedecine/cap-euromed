What you'll find in your area

Your dashboard

Questions covered, mistakes to review, mock exam results: you know where you stand every day, and what's left to do.

Practice, with corrections

For each question, you mark every statement true or false, then check your answers. The right answer appears, and your mistakes go to "To review" so you can come back to them.

Mock exams, just like test day

100 questions, 4 statements per question and the clock. At the end, your score against the admission threshold, and the detail of every mistake.

And our team follows your progress: we see where you stand and focus the revision video calls on your weak points.
